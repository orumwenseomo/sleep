The bear case rests on (1) competitive entry into AEC DSPs (Marvell's "Golden Cable" program with Amphenol/Luxshare/TE/3M/Broadex, Broadcom's BCM87851 AEC PHY) plus Credo's March 2026 patent settlements that license Amphenol/Molex/TE/Volex into the category, (2) visible margin erosion (GAAP GM 68.2% → 64.5% QoQ; industry commentary that AEC GMs settle at 62–65% as competition arrives), (3) extreme customer concentration (top-10 ≈ 90% of FY26 revenue; top-3 ≈ 74% in Q1 FY27 per Fool, 88% in Q3 FY26 per an earlier note), and (4) Nvidia keeping copper in-rack for Rubin/Rubin Ultra but pushing NVLink to optics/CPO out-of-rack and going "native optical NVLink" with Feynman (2028). No source found evidence of an announced hyperscaler AEC loss, confirmed Amazon dual-sourcing, or a published short report; short interest is only ~3.7% of float.
