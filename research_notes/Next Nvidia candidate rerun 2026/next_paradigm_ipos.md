### Inferences
- The price collapse is at the model/API layer, not the compute layer: token prices -50% while GPU-hour prices rise. That squeezes model labs' gross margins and favors capacity owners (neoclouds, power/shell providers) and application companies whose COGS is tokens (Duolingo's margin raise is a direct example).
- The Sept 14 "slow down AI" episode is the first 2026 instance where the demand side (compute spend) was questioned from inside the labs; it did not show up in Q2 reported numbers.
